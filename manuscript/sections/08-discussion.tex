\paragraph{Observe the lifecycle.} Our phone runs turn the cached-app freezer on (\S\ref{sec:phys-setup}) because it was off by default on \PhysFreezerOffTrialUnits Pixel~8 and \PhysSamsungFreezerOffTrialUnits Galaxy~S25 units behind the reported trials; even with it on, the S25 froze a cached client in \PhysSamsungFrozen attempts within \PhysSamsungFreezeWindowS\,s. Units that keep the freezer off do not hold callbacks this way; we did not test whether Samsung's own freezing policy holds them over longer windows or with the screen off. The deferred class depends on the vendor's provider as well: Samsung's Contacts provider marks its notifications with a hidden no-delay flag (\PhysSamsungNoDelay first callbacks), so a background client heard them after \PhysSamsungBgMedian while MediaStore stayed deferred (Table~\ref{tab:phone-results}). A client therefore must not assume its deployment's lifecycle or callback delay; Step~1 of Table~\ref{tab:guidance} observes them at run time, and without that check every gap must be treated as one that may lose coverage.

\paragraph{Derived outputs.} Reusing an output for equal inputs is safe only if the input keys capture every dependency: a draft with current structured fields can still carry stale text from context outside the keys, and the task oracle (\S\ref{sec:agent-tasks}) does not check whether the text reflects the new facts.

\paragraph{External validity.} Emulators on one host, with scripted mutations, supply the source map, policy campaigns and counterexamples. Physical results come from two rented models, \PhysDevice and Galaxy~S25, of one Android release; the S25 ran only the callback and race tests, on \PhysSamsungUnits units. Under the same seeded schedule, the S25's Contacts and MediaStore stamps matched the Pixel~8 trial for trial (\PhysSamsungRaceSameAsPixel), and its MediaStore callbacks were deferred alike, but Contacts delivery and freezing differed, so other vendors and releases may differ again. Where the Pixel~8 differs from the emulators (slower foreground and assistant callbacks, a longer check-to-effect interval, finer file mtimes), we report both; experiments not run on a phone keep emulator results (\S\ref{sec:phys-setup}). The rented phones stay on the charger and their Doze is forced, so we have measured neither how often gaps occur in real use nor battery drain on a physical phone. Selected query inspection is not population coverage. Ambiguous temporal overlaps are not counted as successes.

\paragraph{Platform opportunities.} Useful APIs would return values and sequence counters atomically, expose reset-safe epochs and complete changes including deletions, and support conditional effects. Public counters alone would not repair an inconsistent observation. Such APIs could make reuse worthwhile for expensive sources.
