\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
The evidence map supports the deployment-specific choices in Table~\ref{tab:guidance}. Fast notification delivery reduces invalidation delay but supplies no synchronous freshness guarantee.

\paragraph{External validity.} All runtime evidence comes from emulators on one host, with scripted mutations. Physical/OEM replication, energy measurements and field overlap rates remain open. The summed-call model and measured draft tasks have different workloads; structured draft goals do not assess language quality or open-ended planning. The cooperative provider is a reference, not a platform patch. Unsupported stock contracts remain disabled, and selected query inspection is not population coverage. Ambiguous temporal overlaps are not counted as successes.

\paragraph{Platform opportunities.} Useful APIs would return values and sequence counters atomically, expose reset-safe epochs and complete changes including deletions, and support conditional effects. Public counters alone would not repair an inconsistent observation. Such APIs could make reuse worthwhile for expensive sources, but the present measurements do not establish a general performance advantage.
