\paragraph{Observe the lifecycle.} Our Pixel~8 runs turn the cached-app freezer on (\S\ref{sec:phys-setup}) because it was off by default on \PhysFreezerOffTrialUnits units behind the reported trials. On the Galaxy~S25 it was off on \PhysSamsungFreezerOffUnits units, and even with it on a cached client froze in \PhysSamsungFrozen attempts. On such devices the held-until-thaw class may not occur in the field. The deferred class depends on the vendor's provider as well: Galaxy~S25 Contacts notifications carried a hidden no-delay flag (\PhysSamsungNoDelay first callbacks), so a background client heard them after \PhysSamsungBgMedian while MediaStore stayed deferred (Table~\ref{tab:phys-samsung}). A client therefore must not assume its deployment's lifecycle or callback delay: it should check the freezer and Doze state at run time, or treat every gap as one that may lose coverage.

\paragraph{Derived outputs.} Reusing an output for equal inputs is safe only if the input keys capture every dependency: a draft with current structured fields can still carry stale text from context outside the keys, and the task oracle (\S\ref{sec:agent-tasks}) does not check whether the text reflects the new facts.

\paragraph{External validity.} Emulators on one host, with scripted mutations, supply the source map, policy campaigns and counterexamples. Physical results come from two rented models, \PhysDevice and Galaxy~S25, of one Android release; the S25 ran only the callback and race tests, on \PhysSamsungUnits units. The two models agreed on the race and on MediaStore deferral but differed in Contacts delivery and freezing, so other vendors and releases may differ again. Where the Pixel~8 differs from the emulators (slower foreground and assistant callbacks, a longer check-to-effect interval, finer file mtimes), we report both; experiments not run on a phone keep emulator results (\S\ref{sec:phys-setup}). The rented phones stay on the charger and their Doze is forced, so we have measured neither how often gaps occur in real use nor battery drain on a physical phone. Selected query inspection is not population coverage. Ambiguous temporal overlaps are not counted as successes.

\paragraph{Platform opportunities.} Useful APIs would return values and sequence counters atomically, expose reset-safe epochs and complete changes including deletions, and support conditional effects. Public counters alone would not repair an inconsistent observation. Such APIs could make reuse worthwhile for expensive sources.
