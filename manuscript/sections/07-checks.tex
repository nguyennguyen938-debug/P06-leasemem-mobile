\paragraph{Anchoring counterexamples.} A separate mutator writes once between the optimistic fact read and the watermark assignment in \RevStock stock-source trials, on a seeded schedule that cycles through update, delete and insert. Each outcome is fixed by source and write kind, so the counts are counterexamples, not frequencies. Every update or insert to a source whose stamp certifies yielded a value/version pair that certified the stale value as \Fresh (emulators: \PhysRaceEmuStampedStale; Pixel~8: \PhysRaceStampedStale\ifPhysSamsung; Galaxy~S25: \PhysSamsungRaceStampedStale\fi), every delete changed the version map and was caught (Pixel~8: \PhysRaceDeletesCaught), and sources without a certifying stamp gave \Unknown (\PhysRaceStamplessUnknown; Table~\ref{tab:phys-race}).\ifPhysSamsung{} The S25 ran the same schedule and matched the Pixel~8 trial by trial (\PhysSamsungRaceSameAsPixel by source, trial and seed): the race reproduces on a second vendor's phone, which is not an independent rate.\fi{} Strict \sys returns \Unknown because the stock contract is unestablished. A rapid-call-log campaign makes \RevTickPairs paired edits/reads in \RevTickTrials trials, finding \RevTickHits equal-tick value changes. This negative result cannot establish strict timestamp advancement. Another \RevSupplement trials check fallback for legacy/missing certificates, permission loss, clock restoration, same-metadata file edits, SMS identifier reuse and Contacts reset; failed attempts remain available.

\paragraph{Cooperative conformance.} The transactional reference provider is a one-row positive control. It runs \RevReference trials over controls, observation/re-anchoring races, concurrent reads, exhaustion, deletion/reuse, reset/restore, ABA, query/support/delta boundaries and the action interval. The optimistic schedule yields \RevReferenceRace stale anchors. The client accepts an attempt when its before and after tokens match; the independent oracle also checks that all five snapshots of that attempt carry the same epoch and sequence number in timestamp order, and recomputes every verdict. A control attempt took a median of \RefBracketMs\,ms. All conform, including unchanged positive controls and \Unknown on reset/exhaustion. Because the provider returns value, epoch and sequence number in one transaction, this checks the protocol implementation, not a stock provider. In \RevActionResidual trials, a write after certification precedes the call-log effect, demonstrating the remaining action race. These injected schedules do not estimate its natural frequency.

\paragraph{Matched host transport.} Every arm shares a loopback channel forwarded over adb, serialization, permissions and FGS residency, with separate caches and randomized order (\RevHost paired trials, 30 per platform/write group; Table~\ref{tab:checks-host}, appendix). In this client the validate arm re-reads every fact exactly as refresh does and adds one strict check per fact that returns \Unknown without reading the provider. Its near-zero difference from refresh therefore follows from the code and is not evidence for or against validation. The omitted base-input arm also equals refresh. The relay re-reads the facts whose source a delivered event flagged, and every fact after an epoch change or without a cached value, so it is cheaper but depends on a live helper and asynchronous events. All quiet results match post-trial values. With continuous writers, later-value differences have ambiguous timing and are not counted as stale-at-certification failures. \ifPhysHost On the phone, a batched \stamp arm took \PhysHostStampGainNone, \PhysHostStampGainOne and \PhysHostStampGainContinuous\,ms less than refresh per batch with no, one and continuous writes (\PhysHostTrials trials; Table~\ref{tab:phys-host}). It is faster because it re-reads fewer facts, from \PhysHostReadsStampMin to \PhysHostReadsStampMax per batch against \PhysHostReadsRefresh, and trusts its stamps for the rest: the optimistic policy that certified a stale value after every update or insert in the race (\PhysRaceStampedStale). Refresh itself was fastest under continuous writes, a phone-side difference whose cause we did not measure (Table~\ref{tab:phys-host}).\else The unbatched \stamp result of Table~\ref{tab:host} has not been repeated with batched reads.\fi

\paragraph{Verified deployment diagnosis.} Table~\ref{tab:deploy} records 20 trials per platform/configuration. Median background delay with freezing disabled is \FollowABg on Android~13 and \FollowBBg on Android~15, consistent with the separate ten-trial Android~15 diagnosis (\RevDeployBBg). Restarting the process after changing the timeout prevents a preceding freezability deadline from surviving. The frozen arm verifies its cgroup before mutation and during the hold, and measures callbacks before and after thaw. FGS medians are \FollowAFg and \FollowBFg; bound-assistant medians are \FollowAAssist and \FollowBAssist. Table~\ref{tab:deploy} reports ranges and denominators. This distinguishes the 10-second unfrozen behavior from mixed-state delays in the source and policy campaigns. API-35 FGS-type checks are in Appendix~\ref{app:stamp-based}.
